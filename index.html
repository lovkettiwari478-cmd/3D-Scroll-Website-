04

Chapter 04 · Security

Private
by design.

MANISK is built around a simple rule — your data belongs to you. Privacy isn't a layer added later; it's the foundation the system is being constructed on.

  • 01

    Your data stays yours

    Stored under your control. Never mined, never sold.

  • 02

    Explicit permissions

    MANISK acts only with access you grant — per tool, per task.

  • 03

    Clear boundaries

    What leaves your device stays visible, auditable and revocable.

  • 04

    You stay in the loop

    Automation runs on your terms, with approval where it matters.

Design principles — the standard MANISK is being built to.
